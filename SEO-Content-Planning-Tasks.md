# SEO Content Planning Tasks

Consolidated prompts/tasks from client (ChatGPT Prompts & AI Tips / @Yourchatgptguide). Use each prompt with your own [paste] or [insert keyword/niche] as needed.

---

## 1. Full SEO Strategy Architect

**Prompt:**  
Act as a professional SEO strategist. Based on my business model, target audience, competition level, and goals, design a complete SEO strategy covering keyword focus, content structure, topical authority, internal linking, and growth priorities. Business details: [paste].

---

## 2. Deep Keyword Research

**Prompt:**  
Perform comprehensive keyword research for the niche [insert niche]. Identify primary, secondary, and long tail keywords. Group them into logical clusters based on search intent and topical relevance.

---

## 3. Search Intent Optimization

**Prompt:**  
For the keyword [insert keyword], analyze search intent and define the ideal content format, depth, structure, and user expectation required to rank competitively.

---

## 4. Competitor Reverse Engineering

**Prompt:**  
Analyze the top ranking pages for the keyword [insert keyword]. Identify common structural patterns, content depth, semantic coverage, and content gaps I can improve upon.

---

## 5. SEO Content Blueprint

**Prompt:**  
Create a comprehensive SEO content blueprint for the keyword [insert keyword]. Include title structure, meta description, heading hierarchy, semantic keyword integration, FAQs, and internal linking suggestions.

---

## 6. On Page Optimization

**Prompt:**  
Review the following page content and optimize it for on page SEO. Improve title tags, meta descriptions, heading clarity, keyword placement, readability, and user engagement without over optimization. Content: [paste].

---

## 7. SEO Audit and Refinement

**Prompt:**  
Act as a senior SEO auditor. Evaluate my complete SEO plan including keywords, content structure, linking strategy, and optimization approach. Identify weaknesses and refine it into a sustainable ranking strategy.

---

## Suggested workflow

| Step | Task | When to use |
|------|------|-------------|
| 1 | Full SEO Strategy Architect | Start: define overall strategy from business details |
| 2 | Deep Keyword Research | Define primary/secondary/long-tail and clusters |
| 3 | Search Intent Optimization | Per keyword: format, depth, structure, expectations |
| 4 | Competitor Reverse Engineering | See what ranks and find gaps |
| 5 | SEO Content Blueprint | Per page: titles, meta, headings, FAQs, internal links |
| 6 | On Page Optimization | Apply to existing page content |
| 7 | SEO Audit and Refinement | Final review and refinement of the full plan |
